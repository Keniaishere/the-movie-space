    So what’s The Movie Space?

    The Movie Space is built from a master collection of 5,919 films spanning 1902–2025. The Orbit presents 5,872 of them on its 1930–2025 timeline. Each poster sits on a ring that matches its IMDb rating: whole numbers land on the ring, while decimal ratings fall between them.

    Alongside familiar filters such as genre, runtime, awards, and production region, the Galaxy places 4,523 films using language found in audience reviews. A multi-label GoEmotions model groups its full vocabulary into eight disclosed families—love, joy, surprise, anger, fear, disgust, sadness, and neutral—and combines them into valence and arousal coordinates. Love uses lilac; frustration uses burnt orange. These profiles describe patterns in audience response, not objective properties of a film.

    Review evidence combines available TMDB user reviews with the academic IMDb Spoiler Dataset by Rishabh Misra. IMDb reviews are connected to this collection only through exact IMDb title IDs; no title-name guessing is used. For matched films, the analysis uses a representative sample of up to 25 academic IMDb reviews together with available TMDB reviews.

    Confidence labels primarily reflect review quantity: low means 1–4 reviews, medium means 5–9, and high means 10 or more. They indicate the strength of the available review evidence, not certainty that the model’s interpretation is correct. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB, IMDb, or their affiliates.
